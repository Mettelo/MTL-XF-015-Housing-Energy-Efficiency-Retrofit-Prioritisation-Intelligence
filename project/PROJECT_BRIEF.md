# Project Brief

## Client Commission
The authority has a constrained retrofit budget and cannot fund every eligible property. Previous prioritisation has relied too heavily on headline efficiency ratings rather than combining property characteristics, consumption evidence and intervention history.

The team has been commissioned to answer:

> **Which property segments should receive retrofit investment first to maximise defensible energy-efficiency benefit while keeping the prioritisation transparent and fair?**

## Primary Dataset
**National Energy Efficiency Data-Framework (NEED) Anonymised Data 2026 — 50,000-row sample**  
Publisher: Department for Energy Security and Net Zero  
Official source: https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/national-energy-efficiency-data-framework-need-anonymised-data-2026

## Required Delivery Lifecycle
**Discovery → Requirements → Governance → Acquisition → Engineering → Data Quality → Analysis → Decision Framework → BI/Product → Assurance/UAT → Executive Recommendation → Handover**

## Cross-Functional Roles
| Role | Expected contribution |
|---|---|
| Business Analyst | Define investment decision, stakeholder needs, constraints and acceptance criteria. |
| Data Governance Analyst | Govern property fields, quality, lineage and permitted interpretations. |
| Data Engineer | Build reproducible property/consumption transformation pipeline. |
| Housing/Data Analyst | Analyse consumption, property segments and intervention history. |
| BI Developer | Build portfolio and investment-prioritisation decision support. |
| Data Scientist | Develop transparent prioritisation/scenario model where justified. |
| AI/Model Governance Analyst | Assess bias, explainability, human oversight, validation and monitoring. |

## Core Business Questions
1. What is the current baseline and where is material variation concentrated?
2. Which definitions and data-quality issues could change the decision?
3. What segments, organisations, products, areas or cohorts require deeper investigation?
4. Which indicators are appropriate for prioritisation and which are not?
5. What intervention options are supported by evidence?
6. How should management monitor the decision after handover?

## Success Criteria
- traceable requirements and acceptance criteria;
- governed definitions, ownership, lineage and quality rules;
- reproducible ingestion/transformation;
- defensible property characteristics, energy consumption, efficiency measures, intervention patterns, segment prioritisation and scenario comparison;
- transparent prioritisation logic;
- useful decision-support product;
- UAT/assurance evidence;
- executive recommendation and handover;
- visible team contribution.
